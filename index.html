01THE POINT OF THE TRIP

Leave room
for the weather.

Ireland rewards a loose plan. Keep the drive days short, the waterproofs close, and the evenings unbooked enough for one more pint by the harbour.

This route is designed for first-timers who want the iconic coast without collecting checkmarks. Three bases. One big loop. A little room to get lost.

◌Best windowMay—September for longer light
↝Travel rhythm2–3 nights per base
⌁Pack forSun, rain, wind — sometimes together

Pick up the car in Cork or Dublin, then settle into Killarney. Walk the lakeshore at golden hour; the only plan is dinner somewhere warm.

Waterfalls,
without hurry.

Cycle or jaunting-car through the park. Torc Waterfall, Muckross Abbey and a picnic with views across Lough Leane.

Take the long way
to Dingle.

Drive the coast via Inch Beach and Minard Castle. Land in town for oysters, live trad music and an Atlantic sunset.

SCENIC DRIVESleep: Dingle

SLEA HEADLOOP · 55 KM

Edge of the
known world.

Follow Slea Head Drive to Dunquin Pier, Coumeenoole Beach and the Blasket Centre. Add a guided sea-kayak if the water is kind.

KAYAK · MODERATESleep: Dingle
05
THE BURREN · GALWAY3 HR · 160 KM

Cliffs, limestone,
then oysters.

Start with the Cliffs of Moher before the crowds, cross the Burren’s lunar landscape, and arrive in Galway for a late table at Aniar.

MUST-SEE · Early startSleep: Galway
06
CONNEMARALOOP · 150 KM

One more wild
horizon.

Take the Sky Road to Kylemore Abbey, pause at Dog’s Bay, and let the drive home take the shape it wants.

COASTAL WALKSleep: Galway

GALWAY → DUBLIN2.5 HR · 210 KM

Leave the coast
slowly.

Breakfast by the Spanish Arch, then take the motorway east. If you have 10 days, add Achill Island or two nights in West Cork.
